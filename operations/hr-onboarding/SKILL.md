## When NOT to Use

## Overview

Employee onboarding is the structured process of integrating new hires into the organization — from offer acceptance through the first 90 days and beyond. A well-designed onboarding program directly impacts first-year retention, time-to-productivity, and long-term employee engagement. Research shows that organizations with standardized onboarding processes improve new hire retention by 82% and productivity by over 50%. This skill covers building repeatable onboarding workflows, managing documentation (offer letters, contracts, NDAs, tax forms, benefits enrollment), coordinating IT/HR/facilities logistics, and tracking milestone completion through the critical first three months.

## Workflow: 30-60-90 Day Plan

A structured onboarding timeline divided into three phases ensures new hires progress from orientation to autonomy.

### Day 0-30: Foundation (Orientation & Setup)

**Pre-arrival (Week -1 to Day 0):**
- Send offer letter and employment contract for e-signature
- Collect tax forms, identity documents, and banking details
- Provision workstation, accounts, email, and tools
- Schedule first-day welcome with team and manager
- Assign onboarding buddy or mentor

**Week 1: Orientation**
- Company overview: mission, values, org structure, culture
- HR and benefits orientation (health insurance, payroll schedule, PTO policy)
- IT setup verification and security training
- Role-specific context: team structure, key stakeholders, communication norms
- Introduction to key tools (Slack, Notion, CRM, project management)

**Week 2-4: Role Immersion**
- Complete role-specific training modules and SOPs
- Shadow team members in core workflows
- Assign first small deliverables with clear success criteria
- Schedule weekly 1:1 check-ins with manager
- Set up OKR or performance goals for the first quarter

### Day 31-60: Integration (Contributing with Support)

**Week 5-6: Independent Contribution Begins**
- Take ownership of one recurring task or project
- Begin attending cross-functional meetings
- Build relationships with adjacent teams (sales, product, engineering)
- Complete required compliance training (data privacy, security, industry regulations)

**Week 7-8: Deepening Context**
- Participate in sprint/review cycles relevant to role
- Identify early process improvements or knowledge gaps
- Receive mid-point feedback via structured check-in
- Review progress against 30-day goals, adjust 60-day targets

### Day 61-90: Autonomy (Full Integration)

**Week 9-12: Ownership & Independence**
- Lead a small project or initiative with minimal supervision
- Contribute to team planning and retrospective sessions
- Present a 90-day impact summary to manager and team
- Set goals for the remainder of the first year
- Conduct formal 90-day performance review

**Key Principle:** Each phase should have clear, measurable success criteria. The 30-day marker checks "can they navigate the organization?" The 60-day marker checks "are they contributing independently?" The 90-day marker checks "are they delivering value?"

## Anti-Rationalization Table

| Rationalization | Reality |
|---|---|
| "We do not need SOPs" | Without SOPs, quality depends on memory. Document everything. |
| "Manual processes work fine" | Manual processes do not scale and are error-prone. Automate. |
| "Compliance is optional" | Compliance protects you legally. Build it in from the start. |

## Common Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Impact | Prevention |
|---------|--------|------------|
| Information overload on Day 1 | Overwhelms new hire, reduces retention | Spread orientation across first 2 weeks, use bite-sized modules |
| No pre-arrival setup | New hire sits idle Day 1 without tools/access | Complete IT/account provisioning before start date |
| One-size-fits-all onboarding | Ignores role-specific needs (remote vs office, IC vs manager) | Build role-track templates with shared core + role-specific modules |
| Skipping manager training | Manager doesn't know how to onboard effectively | Provide onboarding manager checklist and training |
| No structured feedback loops | Issues surface at 90-day review when too late | Implement weekly 1:1s and 30/60/90 milestone check-ins |
| Neglecting culture integration | New hire feels isolated, leaves within 6 months | Assign buddy/mentor, schedule cross-team introductions |
| Paper-based processes | Lost forms, manual data entry, compliance risk | Use digital HRIS with e-signature and automated workflows |
| Early departure from structured plan | After first week, onboarding becomes ad-hoc | Maintain structured plan through full 90 days |


## Process

1. **Prepare** — Define role requirements, gather onboarding documents (offer letter, contracts, compliance forms), provision accounts and hardware before start date
2. **Schedule** — Build the 30-60-90 day timeline, book orientation sessions, assign buddy/mentor, send pre-arrival communication
3. **Execute** — Run day-by-day onboarding workflow: Day 1 orientation → Week 1 training → Week 2-4 immersion → Week 5-8 integration → Week 9-12 autonomy
4. **Track** — Monitor milestone completion at 30/60/90 day checkpoints, verify document signatures, collect feedback at each phase
5. **Review** — Conduct formal 90-day performance review, assess time-to-productivity, identify process improvements for next hire

- [ ] Feedback collected and applied to onboarding process improvements

## Monetization

Onboarding automation can be offered as a service to growing companies that lack dedicated HR operations. Revenue models include:

- **Fixed-fee onboarding setup**: One-time engagement to build a client's onboarding workflow, template library, and HRIS integration ($2K-$5K per engagement)
- **Monthly retainer for ongoing management**: Manage new hire cycles for companies hiring 2-10 people/month ($500-$2K/month)
- **Onboarding audit and optimization**: Review existing process, identify gaps, recommend improvements, produce playbook ($1K-$3K)
- **HR tech stack consulting**: Evaluate and recommend HRIS, e-signature, learning management, and compliance tools ($500-$2K)
- **Template marketplace**: Sell reusable onboarding checklists, 30-60-90 day templates, offer letter templates, and SOP libraries ($10-$50 per template, passive income)

Target clients: startups scaling past 10 employees, SMEs without dedicated HR, and remote-first companies needing standardized virtual onboarding.
