## Platform Context

TikTok is the dominant short-video platform in Indonesia. Key patterns:
- **Peak hours:** 19:00-22:00 WIB for highest engagement
- **Content types:** Tutorials, product demos, local humor, challenges
- **Hashtag strategy:** Mix viral + niche + branded hashtags
- **Posting frequency:** 2-3x daily for growth, 1x daily for maintenance
## Overview

Automated TikTok marketing toolkit for the Indonesian market. Covers content posting, carousel creation, and account management with peak-hour scheduling (19:00-22:00 WIB), local hashtag strategies, and multi-account operations.

## Process

1. **Authenticate** — Connect TikTok accounts via PostBridge or browser automation
2. **Prepare content** — Create carousel images/videos with text overlays
3. **Schedule** — Set posting times for peak Indonesian hours (19:00-22:00 WIB)
4. **Apply hashtags** — Mix viral + niche + branded hashtags for discoverability
5. **Engage** — Auto-like, comment, and follow based on engagement rules
6. **Monitor** — Track performance metrics and adjust strategy

## Verification Checklist

- [ ] All accounts authenticated and posting successfully
- [ ] Content posts at scheduled WIB times
- [ ] Carousel renders correctly (multi-slide, text overlays)
- [ ] Hashtag strategy applied (viral + niche + branded)
- [ ] Engagement actions (likes, comments) executing
- [ ] No account flags or bans from automation
