## Platform Context

TikTok is the dominant short-video platform in Indonesia. Key patterns:
- **Peak hours:** 19:00-22:00 WIB for highest engagement
- **Content types:** Tutorials, product demos, local humor, challenges
- **Hashtag strategy:** Mix viral + niche + branded hashtags
- **Posting frequency:** 2-3x daily for growth, 1x daily for maintenance
